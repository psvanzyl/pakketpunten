# Pakketpunten 📦

**Package Pickup Points — Infrastructure & Policy Research**

A project documenting the deployment and DNS configuration for the `pakketpunten.home.arpa` service, supporting research and policy work on parcel delivery point networks in the Netherlands.

## Project Status

- Pi-hole DNS record configured: `pakketpunten.home.arpa`
- Part of the broader homelab infrastructure at `psvanzyl.home.arpa`

## Related

This work supports policy research at the Dutch Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management (IenW) on sustainable logistics and last-mile delivery infrastructure.
